🌍 1. Setting & World

1.1 World Overview

Setting: หมู่บ้านริมทะเลทางตอนใต้ของไทย — A small coastal village with light magical elements woven into everyday life.

1

Magic is natural

Not a separate fantasy world, but magical elements exist within the real village.

2

Spirits are neighbors

Not scary, not otherworldly. The spirits are part of the community.

3

Craft carries memory

Old things have stories. Fabric, pottery, plants — all carry energy.

4

Community is everything

The village survives because people help each other, living and dead.

Area 1: ร้านผ้าโบราณ

The Old Fabric Shop

"Warm amber light, dust motes floating, fabric draped everywhere. Cozy but slightly mysterious."

A centuries-old shop filled with bolts of fabric from around the world. Each piece has a story — a wedding dress from one generation, a temple robe from another. The shop smells of jasmine and old paper.

Magic element: The fabric seems to fold itself sometimes. The loom works without anyone touching it after midnight.
Items (Chains):
ผ้าคราม (Indigo fabric) — Tiers 1-3 ผ้าฝ้าย (Cotton fabric) — Tiers 1-3 ผ้าไหม (Silk) — Tiers 4-6 ดอกไม้ปัก (Embroidered flowers) — Tiers 4-6 ลายฉลุ (Cut-work patterns) — Tiers 7-10

Character: คุณป้ามะลิ (Aunt Mali)

Area 2: สวนริมคลอง

The Canal Garden

"Green and fresh, morning mist, the sound of water. Peaceful and grounding."

A lush garden along a calm canal. Morning glories climb bamboo trellises, lotus flowers float in the water, and a small temple sits under a banyan tree. Butterflies and dragonflies everywhere.

Magic element: The plants grow faster than they should. Sometimes a dragonfly lands and you hear a whisper.
Items (Chains):
ดอกมะลิ (Jasmine flowers) — Tiers 1-3 ดอกกระถิน (Acacia flowers) — Tiers 1-3 ใบไผ่ (Bamboo leaves) — Tiers 4-6 ผลไม้ (Tropical fruits) — Tiers 4-6 ธูปชูชก (Incense sticks) — Tiers 7-10

Character: พี่แดง (Daeng)

Area 3: บ้านของผีเล็ก

The Friendly Spirits' Home

"Warm candlelight, soft shadows, friendly presence. Like being welcomed into someone's home where you can't quite see the host."

An old wooden house where a family of small spirits live. Not abandoned — the spirits keep it clean, polish the brass, tend the small altar. The house smells of incense and watermelon.

Magic element: Small things move when you're not looking. Offerings disappear. You feel watched — but kindly.
Items (Chains):
ถ่าน (Charcoal) — Tiers 1-3 เทียน (Candles) — Tiers 1-3 ธูป (Incense) — Tiers 4-6 กระถาง (Pottery) — Tiers 4-6 ภาชนะเก่า (Antique dishes) — Tiers 7-10

Character: ผีเอื้อ (Spirits)

PROTAGONIST

มะลิ (Mali)

Young woman, mid-20s

A young woman who studied design in Bangkok and worked there for a couple of years. Now she's back in her home village — called back by her aunt's letter that said simply: "The shop needs you."

Appearance: Medium height, warm brown skin, hair in a practical ponytail (sometimes messy), wears casual clothes — t-shirt, jeans, comfortable sandals. Has a designer's eye — notices details, color, texture. Carries a small sketchbook.
Personality: Observant and curious, warm but slightly guarded (just returned, adjusting), hardworking, follows through, has a dry sense of humor. Initially confused about why she's really back, eventually discovers her own answer.
Arc (MVP):
  • Ch1: Return and reconnect
  • Ch2: Discover the shop's secrets
  • Ch3: Make the village her own
  • Ch4: Embrace her role
  • Ch5: Choose to stay (for now) and help the village
What she wants (stated): To help Aunt Mali with the shop
What she wants (unstated): To find her place, to understand why she left, to decide if she belongs here or in the city

Supporting Cast

AREA 1

คุณป้ามะลิ (Aunt Mali)

Early 70s

Role: Owner of the Old Fabric Shop, Mali's aunt who raised her. Kind but secretive. Knows more than she tells. Has a gentle humor. Treats everyone — living and not — with equal respect.

Arc: Has kept the shop running for decades, knows about the spirits, has been waiting for the right moment to call Mali back — but won't say why.

What she's hiding: Why she really called Mali back. The shop's connection to something older. Her own history with the spirits.

AREA 2

พี่แดง (Daeng)

Early 30s

Role: Tailor, works at the shop. Hardworking, nature-loving, quiet but present. Good with his hands. Notices things others miss.

Appearance: Taller than Mali, lean build, short dark hair, wears white shirts (easy to keep clean while working), has ink stains on his fingers from sketching designs. No-nonsense but gentle.
Arc: Has worked at the shop since he was young. Has his own relationship with the spirits. Sees Mali as both a friend and a complication.

Relationship to Mali: Friend from childhood, now coworker. There's an unspoken history — they were close as kids, then grew apart when she left for Bangkok.

VILLAGE

น้องปิ่น (Pin)

8-10 years old

Role: Village child, follows Aunt Mali everywhere. Curious, fearless, believes in everything. Aspiring spirit expert. Tells everyone about the ghosts she sees (everyone thinks she has a good imagination).

Appearance: Small, messy hair (always seems to be running somewhere), big eyes, bright face, wears shorts and t-shirts, bare feet. Always has some small treasure in her pockets.
Arc: Has actually been seeing spirits since she was tiny. She's not scared because Aunt Mali taught her they're just neighbors. Her "imaginary friend" is ผีเอื้อ.

Relationship to Mali: Thinks Mali is the coolest because she came from "the city." Wants to show her all the spirit spots.

ELDER

ลุงสมหมาย (Uncle Somchai)

Late 60s

Role: Bird seller, village elder. Superstitious, believes in fortune-telling and auspicious days. But genuinely kind underneath. Tells the same stories over and over. Has a pet parrot that mimics him.

Appearance: Tall and stooped, wears a worn hat, has birds in cages on his cart, walks with a stick, laughs a lot.
Arc: Has been predicting fortunes for decades. His predictions are usually wrong but somehow people still believe him. Secretly knows more about the village's history than he lets on.

What he believes: That the shop is protected by something old. That Mali's return was predicted. That the spirits chose her.

MARKET

คุณนายวิไล (Nai Vilai)

Late 40s

Role: Market vendor, gossip center. Talkative, well-meaning, always knows everyone's business. Sells vegetables and knows what everyone is eating for dinner. Can be trusted with secrets (but not with time).

Appearance: Round and warm, colorful market clothes, always moving — stirring, arranging, gesturing, has a voice that carries.
Arc: The village's information network. She doesn't create drama — she just knows about it first. Her gossip has saved the village more than once.

What she knows: Everyone's business, including things Aunt Mali has told her in confidence. She knows why Mali was really called back — but promised not to tell.

SPIRITS

ผีเอื้อ (Phi Eua) — The Friendly Spirits

Very old (no one knows how old)

Role: House guardians, inhabitants of Area 3. Shy but helpful. Polite to a fault. Can't say no to a request. Kind in a quiet way. Have their own hierarchy and customs. Love watermelon.

Appearance: Small (about half Mali's height), translucent, soft glow, simple clothes — like old photographs, wings that fold away when not in use, big eyes, gentle faces.
Arc: They were here before the house was built. They've been protecting the village in small ways — moving things, warning people in dreams, keeping the bad spirits out. They've been watching Mali since she was born.

What they want: To help, but they can't directly intervene in human affairs. They can only guide. Their main job: protect the village, and the shop, and now — Mali.

Chapter 1: กลับมาบ้าน

Coming Home

Opening state: Mali receives a letter from Aunt Mali asking her to come home. The letter says "The shop needs you" but nothing more. Mali, who left the village for Bangkok years ago, isn't sure what to expect. But she goes.
Events:
  • Mali arrives at the village, finds it changed and unchanged
  • She meets คุณนายวิไล at the market (gossip intro)
  • She goes to the shop, is surprised by its condition — beautiful but tired
  • Aunt Mali is happy to see her but doesn't explain much
  • She meets พี่แดง again (childhood friend reunion)
  • She meets น้องปิ่น (who immediately declares her spirit expertise)
New game elements unlocked:
  • Area 1 (ร้านผ้าโบราณ)
  • 3 chains (ผ้าคราม, ผ้าฝ้าย, ดอกมะลิ)
  • NPC Orders system
⚡ Cliffhanger ending: Mali finds a letter hidden in the shop — not addressed to her, but from someone years ago. "If she's the one the shop chose, she'll understand." Who wrote this? What does the shop "choose"?

Chapter 2: ความลับของผ้าเก่า

The Secret of Old Fabric

Opening state: Mali is settling into village life, helping at the shop. She's noticed small things — fabric folding itself, the loom working at odd hours. She's trying not to be weird about it.
Events:
  • Mali starts taking orders from villagers
  • She notices patterns in what people ask for — specific fabrics for specific reasons
  • น้องปิ่น shows her where the spirit house is (Area 3) — "that's where ผีเอื้อ lives"
  • Mali meets the spirits for the first time (brief, they hide)
  • Aunt Mali tells a story about the shop's founding (but leaves out the ending)
  • Mali finds old photographs of her mother at the shop — her mother never mentioned this
New game elements unlocked:
  • Area 1 expanded (more decoration slots)
  • 2 additional chains (ผ้าไหม, ดอกไม้ปัก)
  • Daily orders unlocked
⚡ Cliffhanger ending: Mali finds a piece of fabric hidden behind the old photographs. It's embroidered with the same symbol that was on the letter she found. When she shows Aunt Mali, her aunt's reaction is — not surprise, but something like relief. "It's time," Aunt Mali says. "Time for what?" Mali asks. Aunt Mali doesn't answer.

Chapter 3: สิ่งที่คลองเล่า

What the Canal Says

Opening state: Mali has been at the shop for a few weeks. She and Aunt Mali are working together on a big order — someone in the city wants traditional wedding fabric, and the shop was chosen. Mali is excited and nervous.
Events:
  • Mali works on the wedding order with พี่แดง
  • She spends more time in Area 2 (สวนริมคลอง), helping with the garden
  • น้องปิ่น reports seeing "a shadow in the canal" — most people dismiss it
  • Mali investigates, finds evidence of something
  • The spirits in Area 3 seem agitated — they warn her in their subtle way
  • Mali discovers that the shop has always been connected to the canal — it's an old trade route
  • Aunt Mali finally tells her something real: the shop is a "thread point" — a place where the spirit world and human world are thin
New game elements unlocked:
  • Area 2 (สวนริมคลอง)
  • 5 chains (ผ้าฝ้าย, ดอกกระถิน, ใบไผ่, ผลไม้, ธูป)
  • Weekly events unlocked
⚡ Cliffhanger ending: น้องปิ่น runs to Mali, breathless. "The canal is glowing," she says. "Something is coming."

Chapter 4: ผีช่วยงาน

The Spirits Help Out

Opening state: Something has definitely shifted. The canal glowed last night, and the village is nervous. But the next morning, the wedding order is due — and the shop is behind. Mali is panicking.
Events:
  • Mali works through the night on the order
  • The spirits (ผีเอื้อ) quietly help — moving fabric, holding things in place
  • Mali doesn't see them directly but knows they're there
  • พี่แดง stays to help — they talk about what the shop means to them
  • The order is completed, delivered, the customer is happy
  • Aunt Mali tells Mali the full story: the shop has protected the village for generations. The spirits here aren't just ghosts — they're guardians. And Mali was called back because she has "the thread" — the ability to see and work with them
New game elements unlocked:
  • Area 3 (บ้านของผีเล็ก)
  • 7 chains (including ผ้าไหม, ลายฉลุ, เทียน, ธูป)
  • Storage expansion available
⚡ Cliffhanger ending: Mali is processing all of this. She's not sure she believes it, but the spirits helped, and that was real. Aunt Mali gives her a choice: "You can go back to Bangkok. The shop will find someone else. Or you can stay, and I'll teach you everything." Mali says she needs time to think.

Chapter 5: งานรื่นเริง

The Festival

Opening state: The village is preparing for its annual festival — a celebration of the canal, the spirits, and the community. Mali has been working hard, the shop is thriving, and she's starting to feel like she belongs. But she's still thinking about Aunt Mali's offer.
Events:
  • The village prepares for the festival (everyone has a role)
  • Mali helps decorate Area 1, 2, and 3
  • Old friends and neighbors come to visit
  • คุณนายวิไล tells Mali stories about the festival growing up
  • ลุงสมหมาย predicts (wrong, as always) that something special will happen
  • น้องปิ่น is SO EXCITED about the festival spirits (the real ones)
  • The spirits are preparing too — they have their own role in the festival
Festival scene (central event):

The village comes together. Mali participates in the traditional activities. At night, there's a lantern ceremony. Mali floats a lantern with a wish. The spirits appear (briefly, to those who can see them) — they also have wishes. Aunt Mali watches Mali with pride.

Ending: Mali hasn't given her final answer yet about staying. But in this moment, watching the lanterns float away, she realizes: she doesn't have to choose between here and there. She can be both. She can stay and still be who she is.
Final scene: Mali back at the shop the next morning. She's not leaving. She looks at Aunt Mali and says: "Teach me."

3.2 Post-MVP Cliffhangers (Chapters 6-7)

Chapter 6 (post-MVP)

Someone comes to the village — someone who knows about "thread points." They want to buy the shop. Aunt Mali says no. But this person doesn't take no for an answer.

Chapter 7 (post-MVP)

The village's old enemy — another spirit faction that doesn't believe in coexistence — is making moves. The spirits are worried. Mali and the spirits must work together to protect the village.

✨ 6. Tone Guide

✓ DO

  • Warm, cozy, inviting
  • Light mystery (not dark)
  • Community and belonging
  • Humor in everyday moments
  • Spirits as friends, not foes
  • Thai-inspired aesthetics as accent

✗ DON'T

  • Horror, violence, graphic imagery
  • Dark or depressive themes
  • Cultural appropriation
  • Exclusionary representation
  • Heavy exposition (show, don't tell)
  • Making specific Thai cultural claims

Reference

Gossip Harbor (narrative warmth + mystery) + Seaside Escape (cozy + community)
